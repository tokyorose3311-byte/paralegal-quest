⚖️ Privacy Policy

Paralegal Quest: Civil Litigation Adventure (Android)

Effective Date: July 2, 2026  ·  Last Updated: July 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Paralegal Quest ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Paralegal Quest: Civil Litigation Adventure mobile application (the "App"), available on Google Play. By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account information. If you create an account or sign in, we collect the credentials you provide, such as an email address, display name, or username, through Firebase Authentication.
  • Leaderboard entries. If you participate in leaderboards, we collect your chosen display name, school or team name (if provided), and game scores so they can be displayed to other players.
  • License or access codes. If your access to the App is provided through a classroom, school, or site license, we may process the license code you enter to verify eligibility.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Gameplay data. Scores, progress, quiz results, and in-game achievements, used to save your progress and operate leaderboards.
  • Device and diagnostic data. Our service providers (such as Google Firebase) may automatically collect device identifiers, device type, operating system version, crash logs, and general usage analytics to keep the App functioning and secure.

We do not collect precise location data, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, or financial information within the App. Any purchases of licenses are processed by third-party payment processors outside the App and are subject to their own privacy policies.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate sign-ins;
  • To save your game progress and display scores on leaderboards;
  • To verify classroom, school, or site licenses;
  • To maintain, improve, and secure the App, including diagnosing crashes and technical issues;
  • To respond to support requests; and
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your information for third-party advertising.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect information as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore / Realtime Database, and related services), operated by Google LLC — Firebase Privacy and Security and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services, required for distribution and updates through the Google Play Store — Google Privacy Policy.

4. Leaderboards and Public Display

Leaderboards are a shared feature of the App. If you submit a score, your display name, school or team name (if provided), and score may be visible to other users of the App. Please choose a display name you are comfortable sharing publicly. We encourage students and educators not to use full legal names as display names.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain account and gameplay data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App's features. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by:

  • Using the account deletion option within the App (where available); or
  • Contacting us at the email address in the Contact section below with the subject line "Data Deletion Request."

Upon a verified request, we will delete your account information, leaderboard entries, and saved progress within 30 days, except where retention is required by law. Some information may persist in backups for a limited period before being permanently removed.

6. Data Security

We use commercially reasonable safeguards to protect your information, including Firebase's built-in security rules, encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and restricted administrative access. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is an educational tool designed for paralegal students, legal studies programs, and adult learners. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

Where the App is used in an educational setting with students under 18, we encourage instructors and administrators to have students use non-identifying display names.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct, or delete the information we hold about you, and the right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

9. International Users

The App is operated from the United States, and information is processed and stored on servers located in the United States through our service providers. If you use the App from outside the United States, you understand that your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date above and, for material changes, provide notice within the App or on this page. Your continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
